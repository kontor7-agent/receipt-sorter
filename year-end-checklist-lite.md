# Year-End Admin Checklist (Lite)

Copy into your notes app, or print. Tick as you go. Organisational only; ask your accountant what applies to you.

## Collect
- [ ] Export bank and card statements for the whole year (PDF or CSV)
- [ ] Download invoices you issued; check numbering has no gaps
- [ ] Gather supplier invoices and receipts (email, apps, paper)
- [ ] Photograph or scan paper receipts; run `receipt_sorter.py`
- [ ] List subscriptions and recurring charges, confirm each has an invoice

## Check
- [ ] Find unpaid client invoices and send reminders
- [ ] Spot-check: any big payment without a matching receipt?
- [ ] Note assets bought this year (laptop, phone, furniture) with date and price
- [ ] Review mileage / travel notes if you keep them

## Close
- [ ] Write down deadlines your accountant gave you, with dates
- [ ] Back up the year folder to a second location
- [ ] Note open questions for your accountant
- [ ] Set up next year's folder and a 10-minute weekly receipt slot
